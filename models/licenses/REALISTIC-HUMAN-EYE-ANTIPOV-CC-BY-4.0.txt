Modifications for AnatomIQ Studio 2.2.14:
- Retained the authored geometry, vertex coordinates, mesh transforms, UVs, and textures.
- Converted the two older KHR_materials_pbrSpecularGlossiness materials to standard glTF 2.0 metallic-roughness PBR materials, including the original 1024x1024 diffuse and normal textures.
- Adjusted the clear outer-eye material to render a subtle transparent reflective surface.
- AnatomIQ duplicates the model at runtime and fits it into the two pre-existing, separately selectable Écorché eyeball positions; these are alignment transforms, not source geometry edits.

Changes indicated as required by CC BY 4.0; original credit and URL remain embedded in the GLB metadata.
