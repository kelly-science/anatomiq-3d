NERVOUS SYSTEM STUDY VIEWS

Source-backed brain, spinal cord and cervical-to-arm nerves. Posterior parietal lobules, deep brain and transparent dura are separate study views. Meninges are hidden by default. Leg anatomy is not part of this update.

NERVOUS · SPECIAL SENSES — EYES

The eyes belong to Nervous / Special Senses. Realistic white sclera with fine vessels, glossy transparent corneal surface, iris and pupil. Select the original Écorché cornea or sclera; no separate Eyes system is needed. Source: Alexander Antipov (CC BY 4.0).

CONNECTIVE TISSUE STUDY VIEWS

Existing Écorché cartilage is reclassified without changing mesh IDs. Native Connective only adds anatomy absent from the existing models: 6 costal-cartilage meshes for ribs 8–10, 2 plantar ligaments, 2 wrist retinacula and 4 interosseous membranes. Existing Écorché discs and Muscular Achilles tendons are preserved without duplication. Knee ACL/PCL and menisci are not in this native set.

LYMPHATIC / IMMUNE ORGAN STUDY

Verified BodyParts3D spleen and thymus only. The atlas has no lymph node, duct, vessel or tonsil geometry. Use the separately labeled regional drainage teaching diagram.

URINARY SYSTEM STUDY VIEWS

BodyParts3D 4.3 urinary anatomy. Renal vessels appear through the existing Cardiovascular layer. Kidney cortex, medulla, pyramids and calyces are not independently modeled in this source.

RESPIRATORY STUDY VIEWS

Lung segments use BodyParts3D 4.3; pulmonary arteries and veins come from your existing cardiovascular layer. No vessels are duplicated in the respiratory GLB.
